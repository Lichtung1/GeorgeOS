Gig posters
===========

Drop this project's media in this folder. It shows up on the project page automatically.

  Pictures:  1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg ... up to 8.jpg   (.png also works)
  Videos:    1.mp4, 2.mp4 ... up to 4.mp4
  YouTube:   youtube.txt  with one YouTube link per line

Numbers set the order. Videos show before pictures.
Keep pictures under ~2 MB and videos under ~25 MB (GitHub refuses files over 100 MB).
For anything already on YouTube, use youtube.txt instead of uploading the video.
